# SOHO Agency — Logo Guidelines

Version 1.0 · 20 October 2026

## Brand idea

The identity turns the letter **S** into a compact system: two opposing movements meet in a controlled central channel. It represents discovery, strategic connection and measurable forward motion without relying on an arrow or infinity symbol.

## Primary assets

- `soho-logo-primary.svg`: preferred light-background lockup.
- `soho-logo-horizontal.svg`: standard header and horizontal placement.
- `soho-logo-stacked.svg`: centred or narrow editorial placement.
- `soho-logo-symbol.svg`: favicon, avatar and compact application.
- `soho-logo-wordmark.svg`: use when the symbol is already established nearby.
- `soho-logo-white.svg`: reverse application on Midnight Ink or photography.

## Colour

| Role | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Primary Orange | `#F26522` |
| Deep Orange | `#C2410C` |
| Midnight Ink | `#141218` |
| Electric Violet | `#6D28D9` |
| Coral | `#EC4899` |
| White | `#FFFFFF` |

Use the orange-symbol/ink-wordmark lockup by default. Use a one-colour version for restricted reproduction. The gradient version is an accent asset, not the default logo.

## Clear space and size

- Minimum clear space: **X**, equal to the wordmark's main stroke thickness, on all sides.
- Minimum horizontal logo width: 120px digital, 30mm print.
- Minimum symbol size: 16px digital. At 16–24px use `favicon.svg`, which has an optically enlarged counter and simplified spacing.

## Backgrounds

- White/Soft Canvas: primary or black logo.
- Midnight Ink/dark photography: white logo.
- Brand Orange: white or black monochrome logo depending on contrast.
- Avoid placing the gradient logo on multicolour photography.

## Typography pairing

- Headlines: Bricolage Grotesque 600–800.
- Body/UI: Plus Jakarta Sans 400–700.
- The logo wordmark is custom vector artwork and must never be retyped with either font.

## Incorrect usage

- Do not stretch, rotate or rearrange the lockup.
- Do not recolour individual letters.
- Do not add shadow, bevel, chrome or glass effects.
- Do not place the logo without the required clear space.
- Do not use the gradient as the default mark.
- Do not recreate the wordmark with live text.

## Restoration

The pre-redesign logo and favicon files are preserved under `public/assets/brand/archive/`.
