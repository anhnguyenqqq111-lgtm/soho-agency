# SOHO Agency — Logo Audit and Selection

## Existing identity audit

The previous brand package contained SVG, PNG and WebP wordmarks at 305 × 120, plus application icons in `app/`. It rendered at 46px in the desktop header, 42px on mobile and 56px in the footer. Its strengths were the established orange cue, readable SOHO name and compatibility with the current palette. Its weaknesses were a relatively tall aspect ratio for navigation, limited compact-symbol recognition, uneven behaviour between raster/SVG variants and no documented clear-space or small-size system.

All original files were copied to `public/assets/brand/archive/` before integration.

## Selection

Concept A is selected. Its broad upper/lower terminals and narrow controlled centre create a recognizable S at favicon size while suggesting two inputs being shaped into one outcome. It works in one colour, does not depend on effects and avoids the generic growth-arrow/infinity vocabulary.

The final lockup pairs Concept A with the custom geometric wordmark developed during Concept B. The wordmark is not live type: every letter is an SVG path. The dedicated favicon version enlarges internal spacing for 16–32px rendering.

## Website fit

- The new horizontal viewBox is 400 × 80, better matched to a 76px/64px header.
- Orange remains the recognition colour and Midnight Ink carries the wordmark.
- The white one-colour asset is used in the dark footer.
- SVG replaces raster logo delivery in navigation and footer.
- Structured data and OG compositions point to the new identity assets.
